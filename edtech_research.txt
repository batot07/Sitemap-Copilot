User Study: SkillForge - Professional Online Learning Platform
Participants: 5 Enrolled Students & 3 Instructors

Key Feedback & Observations:
1. Student Experience:
   - "When I log in, I should see 'My Learning' with my current course progress and upcoming assignment deadlines immediately."
   - "The course catalog mixes beginner tutorials with certified masterclasses. We need clear filtering by Skill Level, Topic, and Certification."

2. Assessment & Resources:
   - "Certificates and transcripts are hard to find; they should be grouped under my Student Profile or Accomplishments tab."
   - "Discussion forums feel disconnected from the lecture lessons. Each course module needs its own integrated community tab."

3. Structural Needs:
   - Public View: Course Catalog, Enterprise/Team Training, Pricing, Free Resources.
   - Authenticated Student Portal: My Learning (Active Courses), Saved/Wishlist, Certificates, Community/Forums.
   - Isolated Instructor Portal: Curriculum Builder, Student Grading, Revenue/Payouts.
