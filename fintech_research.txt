User Study: VaultPay - Personal Banking & Investment Platform
Participants: 9 Active Account Holders

Key Feedback & Observations:
1. Transaction Clarity & Quick Actions:
   - "Transferring money to friends or paying a bill should take maximum 2 clicks from the main screen."
   - "Transaction names look like cryptic merchant codes. I need human-readable descriptions, merchant logos, and category tags."

2. Budgeting vs Daily Spending:
   - "The savings vaults and investment portfolios should not be combined with the checking balance. It leads to accidental overspending."
   - "I want an isolated 'Wealth & Analytics' tab to track monthly spending trends, net worth, and savings goals."

3. Security & Card Management:
   - "If I lose my card, freezing it needs to be immediately accessible from the primary interface without digging through five account settings menus."
   - High priority for dedicated Security Controls: Card Lock, PIN change, Biometrics, Dispute Transaction.
