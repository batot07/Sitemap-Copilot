User Study: NutriTrack - Mobile Calorie & Habit Tracker
Participants: 10 Everyday Mobile Users

Key Feedback & Observations:
1. Screen Real Estate & Navigation:
   - "There are too many items on the screen. The bottom bar has 6 tabs and icons are overlapping on smaller phones."
   - "I log food 3 times a day; it needs to be a prominent floating action button or the very first tab."

2. Mental Model & Tabs:
   - "I expect standard tabs: Today (Log), Trends (Progress charts), Community, and Profile."
   - "Settings and connected devices (like Apple Health / Fitbit) should simply be inside my Profile page, not on the main bar."

3. Hierarchy Rules:
   - Strictly limit bottom navigation to 4 main destinations: Home/Log, Insights, Recipes, Profile.
   - Modals/sheets for logging food entries rather than deep multi-page jumps.
