User Study: FlowPulse - B2B Team Collaboration SaaS
Participants: 6 Product Managers & 4 Team Leads

Key Feedback & Observations:
1. Daily Workflow Obstacles:
   - "When I log in, I want to land directly on 'Active Projects' and 'My Tasks'. Right now, it defaults to a company newsfeed nobody reads."
   - "Switching between client workspaces takes too many clicks. The workspace switcher should be in the top bar."

2. Admin & Settings Clutter:
   - "Billing details, team seat management, and API integrations are scattered everywhere. They need to live under a dedicated Organization Settings area."
   - "Only admins need to see billing. Standard contributors are confused by inactive billing tabs on their dashboard."

3. Structural Needs:
   - Clear distinction between Workspace Tools (Projects, Analytics, Tasks, Documents) and Admin Controls (Team Roles, Integrations, Billing, Audit Logs).
   - Dedicated hub for Automation & Webhooks accessible only to Tech Leads.
